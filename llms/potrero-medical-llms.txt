# Potrero Medical (Accuryn Medical)

> Predictive-health medical device company in Hayward, California, founded 2012. Its
> FDA-cleared Accuryn Monitoring System converts a Foley catheter into a critical-care
> sensor that automatically measures urine output, intra-abdominal pressure and core body
> temperature, so clinicians can detect acute kidney injury, sepsis and abdominal
> compartment syndrome earlier while reducing CAUTI risk. Following a restructuring the
> company operates as Accuryn Medical; potreromed.com now 301s to accuryn.com.

## No public API

Potrero Medical / Accuryn Medical publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-26 there is no
developer portal, no API reference, no SDK, no webhook catalog and no machine-readable
contract (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection or .proto) anywhere on the
company's surface. Do not attempt to call an Accuryn API; none is documented to exist.

## Device data integration

The Accuryn monitor reaches the electronic medical record through **Capsule** medical device
integration (a third-party medical device information platform), not through a first-party
Accuryn API. An integrator connecting Accuryn to an EMR works through Capsule and the
hospital's interface engine, so the contract that matters is Capsule's, not Potrero's.

## Regulatory

- FDA 510(k) K153655 (April 2016) — original Accuryn Monitoring System clearance
- FDA 510(k) K221020 (January 2023) — subsequent clearance
- Catheter kits packaged and distributed by Medline Industries; the monitor is distributed
  by Potrero Medical.

No published pricing or plans. Capital equipment plus disposable catheter kits, sold through
